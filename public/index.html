A citizen gets a Unified Health ID the first time they present at any connected hospital in Bangladesh. That number follows them for life — so a doctor in Chattogram sees the allergy recorded in Sylhet and the prescription written in Savar.

Network

Connected hospitals

Medical colleges, upazila health complexes and private hospitals on one record. A patient registered at any of them is known to all of them.

Directory

Doctors across the network

Search the whole country, not one hospital. Fees are listed up front; free slots are computed from each doctor’s schedule once you sign in to book.

Appointments

Booking, without the queue

Free slots are worked out from the doctor’s weekly schedule, minus their leave, minus what is already booked — so a slot you are shown is a slot that exists.

    In an emergency, do not wait for a login

    Call first. A casualty doctor can open a record under break-glass access without prior consent — every such read is written to the access log by a database trigger and flagged for review.
