UHID

Demo video: voice-over script

The 2-minute cut on the Clipchamp timeline (project “UHID demo video”), 1:45.83 long. Twelve parts, about 215 words.

How to read it
  • Start each line when its part starts. Watch the time in Clipchamp, or record one part at a time.
  • Each line is written to fit its part at a calm pace, about 2½ words a second. If you run over, drop the part in brackets.
  • The video already shows a title for each part in the lower left. You don’t have to read it out.
  • Say “UHID” as letters: U-H-I-D.
